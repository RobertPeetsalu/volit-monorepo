## Reviewing a project {#reviewing-a-project}

Problems and projects have no comments section. To direct citizens away from high frequency and low quality personal interactions and toward low frequency and high quality on-topic expression of opinions, every citizen can leave one **review** per every Proposed version of every project. 

Each review is one of these types: 

* Support 

* Conditionally support 

* Oppose 

* Inform 

* Legal \- only permissioned government lawyers can post a Legal type review. It is displayed before any other reviews. The lawyer can also set a special flag, that signals to the liquid democracy app that the project version requires constitutional changes that need a supermajority support before the project is Enacted. 

Citizens can edit their review and its type as long as the project is in the Proposed state.
